SECTION 01 // HOW IT WORKS

Turning 4 Days of Manual Work into Minutes.

Manual attendance checking is slow and prone to errors. When employees forget to clock out or take leave, manual formulas break. Here is how clean automation solves it.

MANUAL CHECKING 4 Days

Manual CSV sorting, copy-pasting VLOOKUPs, checking leaves by hand, and risking missed punches going unnoticed.

→
AUTOMATED SPREADSHEET 0-Touch

Power Query cleans raw machine punches, matches shift rosters, tallies approved leaves, and calculates exact payroll deductions in seconds.

"When managing thousands of employee punches across hundreds of branches, a single missed formula error in shift deductions adds up quickly. A well-designed automated sheet makes sure every punch, absence, and leave is accounted for accurately."

— FEDERICO DUMAGPI // SYSTEMS & HR AUTOMATION ARCHITECT

ATTENDANCE AUDIT CHECKLIST 100% AUTOMATED

The automated model systematically checks every daily record against five essential rules:

  • Late Clock-Ins: Identifies punches past shift start time and calculates late minutes based on grace period rules.
  • Early Clock-Outs: Flags any employee departing before their scheduled shift conclusion.
  • Missed Punches (One-Punch Only): Catches instances where an employee clocked in but forgot to clock out (or vice versa).
  • HRMS Leave Tallying: Automatically compares daily absence flags against approved sick, annual, and casual leaves in your HR system.
  • Net Payroll Deductions: Calculates clean hour and salary deduction figures ready for payroll entry.
